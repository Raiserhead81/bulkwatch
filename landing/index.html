Common questions

Before you ask.

How fresh are the positions?
Several AIS sources write into the database around the clock, with a refresh cycle of about five minutes. The figures at the top show how many ships have a live position right now. The real-time feed is part of the licence; the demo shows the stored data.
Which ships are included?
More than 59,000 ships of all types: bulk carriers, container ships, tankers, LNG/LPG, general cargo, RoRo and more. A large share additionally carries owner, manager and classification data, and the numbers grow daily.
Can I keep an eye on my own fleet?
Yes. Put ships on the watchlist, get Telegram alerts on critical changes and use the fleet view for your own units.
Can we present our own fleet with our own photos?
Yes. Set up your company fleet, upload your own ship photos and add condition and outfitting details. From any ship or the whole fleet you produce a finished PDF dossier for charterers, banks and buyers.
How does the licence work?
Per company, sized to your needs: number of users, live AIS, update depth. Price on request, we come back with a concrete offer.
